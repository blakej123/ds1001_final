## Key Findings & Industry Observations

### 1. SportsEngine/PlayMetrics Is The Clear Market Leader
SportsEngine (31% of all 150 programs) is the dominant youth sports registration platform. Its May 2026 acquisition by PlayMetrics creates a combined entity controlling an estimated **40-45% of the identifiable market** across all sports.

### 2. LeagueApps Is The Lacrosse Standard
Across both rounds, LeagueApps captured **~75% of lacrosse programs** with a confirmed platform. Its network effects (referrals, case studies, infographics) have created a moat in the lacrosse category. Basketball and volleyball are more contested.

### 3. Swimming Uses A Separate Ecosystem
Elite swim clubs overwhelmingly use **TeamUnify** (now SportsEngine GoMotion), which predates the travel sports platform wars. TeamUnify was built specifically for aquatics and remains deeply embedded despite corporate consolidation.

### 4. Crossbar Is Hockey-Specific
Crossbar appears almost exclusively in hockey (7/7 confirmed Crossbar programs are hockey). Its white-label subdomain pattern (club.com.app.crossbar.org) makes identification reliable and suggests strong hockey vertical focus.

### 5. Softball Is SportsEngine's Strongest Category
Of the 10 softball programs, 6 (60%) confirmed SportsEngine. Ryzer is emerging as a payment-first alternative specifically for travel softball/baseball.

### 6. Basketball Is Most Fragmented
Basketball has the highest rate of unknown/custom platforms (6/15 in Round 2). Elite grassroots programs (Georgia Stars, Indiana Elite, Seattle Rotary) often self-manage registration using WordPress, PayPal, or custom billing — possibly reflecting their nonprofit/community roots.

### 7. New Sports Added In Round 2
- **Volleyball** → SportsEngine dominant (8/15); custom portals for elite boutique clubs
- **Softball** → SportsEngine dominant (6/10); Ryzer emerging
- **Swimming** → TeamUnify standard (4/5); acquisition by SportsEngine/PlayMetrics may change this

- **Platform confirmed** (via direct URL or search result fingerprint): 79/100 programs
- **Unknown / Custom** (no third-party platform confirmed): 14/100 programs

- Several programs (Western Mass Warriors, Beverly Bandits VB, Carolina Chaos softball) could not be confirmed as distinct organizations or had ambiguous sport classifications
- PlayMetrics' acquisition of SportsEngine (announced May 2026) may render some platform distinctions obsolete; combined entity is referenced as "PlayMetrics" in industry going forward
- TeamUnify is branded as "SportsEngine GoMotion" post-acquisition but retains legacy platform for most swim clubs
